Who Built This

I am Ash.

Someone who has lived the problem, and built a solution that works in real life — not ideal conditions.

I spent a decade in a role that demanded everything. High pressure. Long hours. Shift patterns. No structure outside of work.

My health fell apart. Not because I was lazy. Because I had no system.

I was training sporadically. Sleeping poorly. Stressed about being stressed. So I built The Reset Protocol for myself first — and it worked. Then colleagues asked. Then friends. Then it became clear this was the thing I could actually help with.

I'm not a nutritionist or a therapist. What I am is someone who has lived the problem and built a solution that works in real life, not ideal conditions.

I work with a handful of people at a time — not because I'm exclusive for the sake of it, but because this only works when there's actual coaching involved. When I can adjust. When you're not a number.

I only take clients I believe I can genuinely help. If we're not the right fit, I'll tell you.

Common Questions

Before you apply

01I don't have time

Most people find they have more time — because they're sleeping better and wasting less energy on scattered thinking.

02I'm not fit. Will it be too much?

The Reset Protocol scales to you. If you haven't trained in years, we start there — you progress at your pace.

03What if I miss a workout?

You'll miss things. Everyone does. One missed session doesn't restart the clock — consistency over perfection. We adjust and keep moving.

04Is this just another fitness programme?

No. This is a system for how you live. Movement is part of it. Sleep structure is part of it. Stress management is part of it. It's about function.

05How is this different to other programmes?

You're not following a generic template. You're working with someone who adjusts it to your life, your schedule, your actual situation. The coaching is the difference.

06What if I don't see results?

You will — you're working with a coach who has the knowledge, and who cares.
